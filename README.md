**Hotel Management System**
The Hotel Management System is a comprehensive software solution designed to simplify the management of hotel operations. It automates several key functions, such as room reservations, guest check-ins and check-outs, billing, and room status tracking, which allows hotel staff to work more efficiently and offer a seamless experience for guests. This system aims to reduce the workload of hotel staff by minimizing manual tasks, improving customer service, and optimizing room usage.

**Key Features:**
Customer Management: The system enables hotel staff to maintain a database of customer information. This includes details such as personal information, reservation history, and payment records. Staff can add, update, or delete customer data as needed.

Room Reservation: The system provides a way to manage room bookings in real-time. It checks for room availability and offers options for customers to select the type of room and duration of stay. The system also prevents overbooking, ensuring that only available rooms are booked.

**Check-in and Check-out:** The system manages the check-in and check-out processes for guests. Staff can quickly check in guests, assign rooms, and process payments. Upon check-out, it generates invoices for the total charges, including room rates and additional services.

**Billing and Payment Management:** The system facilitates the creation of bills for guests, detailing charges for their stay and services. It supports different payment methods and keeps track of payments, ensuring accurate financial records.

**Room Availability Tracking:** The system monitors the status of rooms—whether they are vacant, occupied, or reserved—providing real-time information to hotel staff. This feature helps in efficient room management and guest satisfaction.

**Reports and Analytics:** The system generates reports for management, including daily occupancy rates, revenue details, and other performance metrics. These reports help hotel managers in decision-making and forecasting.
